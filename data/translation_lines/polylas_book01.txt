# Unlisted Greek lines take the Polylas line with the same number; `-` means none
# (its sense is folded into a neighbouring line). Only real drifts (Polylas a whole
# line ahead or behind) and clean swaps of two lines are corrected; ordinary
# enjambment, where a phrase crosses the line break, is left alone.
